▸ What the columns mean
ColumnRangeWhat it is
Score−10 … +10 The MAN score — technical only. Ten indicators (Williams %R, EMA crosses, higher-highs, ROC, RSI, two MACD histograms, stochastic, price vs 50-day SMA, EMA21 vs EMA50), each voting +1 or −1, amplified ×1.2. |score| ≥ 8 is the entry threshold.
SCS−1.0 … +1.0 The SNIPER Composite Score — all five layers: technical 0.30 + research 0.30 + fundamentals 0.20 + revisions 0.20, multiplied by a risk overlay. A name can be +10 on Score and weak on SCS: strong technicals, but research, fundamentals or revisions disagree. Read the sign, not the magnitude — magnitude showed r = −0.03 against outcomes over 2,242 closed trades.
Agreement— How the three voters — MAN, Research, Composite — line up. Candidates are ranked by this, best bucket first:
SPLIT they disagree · MAN_OUTLIER / RESEARCH_OUTLIER / COMPOSITE_OUTLIER two agree, one differs · NO_RESEARCH no broker covers this name · NO_COMPOSITE composite produced no score · UNANIMOUS all agree — suppressed, it was the worst bucket in every regime tested.
Rank is meaningful across buckets, not within one: ties break on SCS magnitude, which is noise.
Action— BUY new position, buying strength (bull regime) · BUYi new position, buying weakness — an inverted buy in a bear regime, where the model rates the name Strong Sell and the system bets on the bounce · ADD? signal on a name already held; shown for review, never auto-recorded · SELL exit due today.
Signal From— Which session's data produced the call. On a morning run US signals are still the previous close while EU is today's — highlighted when older than the brief date.
Fee— Estimated round trip at the routed broker, on the INVEST amount beside it. Currency amounts rather than percentages, because a flat minimum commission is ruinous on a small ticket and percentages hide that. French names carry a 0.40% FTT on every buy — a tax, not a broker fee, so no venue avoids it.
Slip— My Real Trades only. (fill − model price) ÷ model price. Positive means you paid more than the model assumed. Check model date first: on a same-day entry this is execution slippage, but on an ADD? for a long-held name it is price drift since the original signal.
